# 000 — Philosophy

ExOS is a computing environment where people and agents work across projects,
workspaces, systems, and personal contexts. Direction, authority, and continuity
survive changes of model, harness, interface, and machine.

Three elements make that true, and they are one relationship seen from three
sides:

- **neo-home** makes ongoing work understandable and actionable.
- **Concierge** preserves its meaning and authority.
- **Harnesses** carry it into the world.

## What endures

> Conversations forget. Handoffs lose it. Models rotate. The product is the
> thing that does not.

Work that outlives its participants is kept as a record of contributions, not as
a transcript. Each contribution has an author, a scope, and a standing. A
correction stays linked to what it corrected; a later observation can unsettle a
claim that once looked secure. Repetition, recency, and a model's confidence
never quietly become a decision.

## Four words

The surface must never let one pass for another:

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Claimed** | Someone owns it. |
| **Running** | Something executes. |
| **Verified** | Evidence supports the outcome. |
| **Accepted** | Someone with authority decided. |

Activity is not progress. A model's narration of its own success is not
evidence. Evidence that something works is not permission to act on it.

## Attention

Take as little human attention as possible, and give as much value as possible.
Interruption is reserved for decisions that need a person. A healthy state takes
no space.

## The surface does not own the work

neo-home shows work and changes it; it does not own it. Workspaces, projects,
environments, spaces, and agents are represented explicitly, so that how a
screen happens to group things never decides what is in scope or who may act.

## Where it stops

Files, deployed systems, and physical outcomes persist on their own terms. The
record keeps the direction and accountability that connect them, and does not
pretend its records are the whole of reality.
