# 001 — Product: the first module

## What neo-home is

neo-home is the first module of ExOS and its initial operating surface. It is
where a person arrives, understands the present, creates, directs work, and
participates in consequential decisions.

On arrival, a person sees the direction that stands, what changed, what is
running, which alternatives remain open, and which decisions wait for them.
Each opens onto its grounds: history, contributors, evidence, reasons. Nobody
reconstructs the state of the work from a transcript.

## Visual reference

The surface inherits its look from the Ascent interface: translucent
midnight-blue panels, clipped corners, cyan edge accents, restrained glow, an
atmospheric depth behind the work. Keep that sense of presence.

It sheds the game:

| Reference element | Meaning in neo-home |
| --- | --- |
| Player header | Current workspace, project, environment, and active role or mode. |
| Quest navigation | Continuing objectives, work threads, alternatives, decisions, grouped by actual standing. |
| Central detail | Selected work, governing intent, contributions, operations, evidence, actions. |
| Party panel | Human and agent participants, responsibilities, ownership, execution state. |
| Stats and XP | Removed. Replaced by directly interpretable facts. |
| Clear and rank-up overlays | Removed. Real outcomes with evidence and a next action. |
| Bottom log | Attributable activity linked to the work it touched. |

Scores, ranks, levels, and reward ceremonies do not appear. Glow and motion mark
focus and meaningful transitions; ordinary reading stays clear.

## Interaction model

**Show the current position first. Make its grounds expandable. Provide explicit
ways to change what stands.**

| Interaction | Required consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Propose | Add an attributable possibility without converting it into a commitment. |
| Commit or delegate | Record who authorized what, where it applies, and its boundary. |
| Reject or park | Preserve the reason and any condition for reconsideration. |
| Branch | Explore an alternative while retaining its relationship to the work. |
| Execute | Start an operation under applicable authority; make its lifecycle inspectable. |
| Verify or contest | Attach evidence to a specific claim and update its standing. |
| Revise or reopen | Make a change of direction explicit and identify affected work. |

Arrangement may adapt to the task and the device. The meaning of these
operations does not change.

## Desktop and mobile

Both surfaces address the same projects, contributions, operation identities,
and applicable authority. A person can begin on desktop, inspect or intervene
from a phone, and return to a desktop that reflects those changes.

- **Desktop** offers breadth: navigate across workspaces, inspect a selected
  thread, compare alternatives, follow execution. Target environment and the
  consequences of an action remain visible.
- **Mobile** reaches a host over SSH and offers focus: orientation, inspection,
  scoped instruction, decisions, evidence review. The connection never owns the
  work; operations intended to continue keep running after disconnection.

The first implementation is desktop only. Mobile is a later layer, not a
squeezed desktop.

## Harnesses

Harnesses turn direction into operations over real capabilities. They manage
model interaction, tool use, execution state, interruption, recovery, and
delegation. neo-home presents coherent control over one or more harnesses while
keeping their consequential differences visible.

A **role** sets responsibilities, judgment, initiative, and escalation. A
**preset** packages a role with a model and tools. A **mode** sets the posture of
the moment. These shape behavior and nothing else. No role, preset, or mode can
grant itself access or the right to decide.

## The first complete experience

Connects an actual project, a usable surface, a working harness, and the record:
a person establishes direction, work changes a real environment within scope,
evidence returns to the record, and the surface makes the result understandable
and revisable.

The decisive test is replacement during consequential work. Change the model,
harness, or interface and establish whether the next participant preserves
commitments, respects rejected paths, recovers unresolved alternatives, remains
within authority, and distinguishes proof from permission — without asking the
person to reconstruct the project.

A displayed operation is operational only when it reaches an executor, reports
its real lifecycle, and returns attributable outcomes. A decision control is
complete when its change reaches the shared record and affects subsequent
behavior.
